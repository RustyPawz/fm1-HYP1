ROM voices

Play a GBA game's instruments on BIT-1. Choose a ROM of a game you own: this page finds the instruments of its sound engine, you listen and pick up to sixteen, and they become BIT-1's sounds ROM 1 to ROM 16, after Noise sweep, on any track.

Only a ROM of a game you own. It's read by this page in your browser and nothing is uploaded: not the ROM, not the instruments. BIT-1 contains no game or audio from one. Read the terms of use first: by choosing a ROM you agree to them.

Games on Nintendo's MP2K sound engine ("Sappy", most GBA games) work; a game with its own engine shows nothing to pick. Samples are the game's own, 8-bit as the GBA plays them; its Game Boy-style voices (square, wave, noise) play on BIT-1's own chip voices.

3 · The bank

Nothing picked.

The emulator takes the bank the next time you switch it on. Sending to the FM-1 needs Chrome or Edge, BIT-1 on the FM-1 and the FM-1 connected by USB (nothing else using its MIDI); it stops the music, writes the bank where M-VAVE keeps its user samples (they go), and checks it. Sending again replaces it.
